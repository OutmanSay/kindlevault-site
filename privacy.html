KindleVault Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Short version: KindleVault runs 100% locally in your browser. We never collect, transmit, sell, or store your Amazon credentials, Kindle library, highlights, notes, or My Clippings.txt files.

1. Data Processing & Local Storage

KindleVault reads highlight content solely from Amazon Kindle Notebook pages you open in your browser or from local My Clippings.txt files you explicitly select. All exported Markdown files, synced book metadata, and settings are stored strictly on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API.

2. Zero Amazon Credentials

KindleVault never asks for, accesses, or stores your Amazon username or password. Pagination requests to Amazon Kindle Notebook run same-origin inside your browser tab using your existing signed-in session.

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you purchase a one-time KindleVault Pro license and click "Activate", the extension sends only your license key and product identifier to Gumroad's verification API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify) to validate your license. No personal reading data or browsing history is included.

4. No Analytics or Third-Party Trackers

KindleVault contains zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising scripts, and zero third-party telemetry.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete individual books from the KindleVault popup at any time, or remove all stored data immediately by uninstalling the extension from Chrome.

6. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support page or Gumroad product page.
